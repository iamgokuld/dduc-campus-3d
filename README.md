# DDUC Campus, But Make It Anime 🏫✨

**A walkable, explodable, anime-styled 3D model of the Deen Dayal Upadhyaya College campus in Sector 3, Dwarka, Delhi, running right in your browser.**

## Why would anyone build this?

> **Visitor:** "Where is the canteen?"
>
> **Student:** "Follow the smell of samosas. Then turn left at the building that's been under 'renovation' since 2019."
>
> **Visitor:** "That's not a map."
>
> **Student:** "It's a lifestyle."

Maps tell you where things are. This tells you where things *are*, from every angle, with the roof taken off. Much more useful when you are lost at 9 AM with a lecture starting in five minutes.

---

## What you can do

- **Orbit view:** spin the whole campus around like a very polite fidget spinner.
- **Explode floors:** pull the buildings apart level by level to see what is hiding inside. Spoiler: mostly classrooms.
- **Street-level look:** drop to a 360-degree view and take in the campus from the ground.
- **Walk around:** use **W A S D** to stroll through the grounds. No queue at the canteen required.
- **Fly to a building:** double-tap a building and get whisked over to it. No bus pass needed.
- **Details toggle:** switch labels and info on or off, depending on how much you enjoy reading signs.

## How it's built

A single HTML page (`index.html`) that uses [Three.js](https://threejs.org/) to draw the whole campus in 3D with an anime-inspired look. No build step, no backend, no drama.

## Run it

- **Easiest:** open the [live site](https://iamgokuld.github.io/dduc-campus-3d/).
- **Locally:** clone this repo and open `index.html` in any modern browser. Double-clicking it works.

Piece 7 · Grounds

Ground 1 and Ground 2

Two open grass fields. Ground 1 is the sandy and grassy field north of the main academic block, near NSS DDUC. Piece 3 · Boys hostel

Boys hostel

A seven-storey brick block with a terrace between the canteen and the girls hostel (the same building, copied as piece 6), its long side running from the back lawn down to the road. The entrance sits in a notch cut out of the road-side corner, with the sitting place and garden in front of it.

Height
7 floors (ground + 6) + terrace, ~23.5 m to the parapet
Plan
~33 × 21 m, traced from the yellow outline on the satellite crop, with a ~9 × 7 m entrance notch
Placed by
GPS stamps on 88 photos taken clockwise round the building from the back, ~88 m east and ~120 m north of the library doors
  • Red-brown brick walls: on the SW side, a sawtooth of five brick units stepping out towards the front from a cream end tower, with deep windows, louvre slots and a stack of cream balcony boxes in every step; on the NE side, plain walls with a cream window strip
  • Cream brick back tower with small deep-set windows; the front face has one deep recessed window bay per floor and a stair tower bulging out, open on every landing behind a brick band and a cranked steel rail
  • Stacked cream balconies with steel top rails and dish antennas at every step of the plan
  • Ground floor under the front: a mess room with its own low sheet roof, a cream door bay and a projecting corner room under a high sheet canopy, then a raised agave planter bulging out under the stair tower, and a covered ramp walkway with handrails running in from the front yard to the glazed lobby
  • Front sitting place: orange benches on white frames along the garden hedge, paved yard with yellow lines
  • Hedge garden with banana plants behind the benches, rows of young mango trees and areca palms on the NE side, shade trees on the SW side
